## 2. Off-Page SEO (How to Rank High)
Google ranks websites based on **Trust**. If other sites link to you, Google trusts you more.

- **YouTube Presence**: In your YouTube video descriptions, add your link at the top: `Website: https://codinginkannada.vercel.app/`.
- **Instagram Bio**: Put your link in your bio. When people click it from Instagram, Google sees "Traffic" and raises your rank.
- **LinkedIn/GitHub**: Add your website link to your social profiles.
- **Google Business Profile**: (Optional) If you have a physical location or "Home Office," you can register on Google Maps.

Google rewards fast sites.
